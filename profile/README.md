## Moriel/Morella Labs

We can have a little hubris as a treat.

After how can Sisyphus be happy without a snack?

How can anyone take a leap of faith only eating humble pie?

To be human is to attempt to defy order and reason itself. 
The essence of humanity is to desire, to want without need. 

To reject hubris is hubris it self. So why not try?
